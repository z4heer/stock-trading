Aapka sawal bilkul sahi hai — program rally start hone se **pehle** hi start ho gaya tha (`10:10 AM`), phir bhi real rally (`62` se `81.25`) miss hui aur loss hua.

Yahan **ek nahi, balki do alag-alag mistakes** hui hain: ek **Business / Risk Logic issue** aur ek **Code / State Logic issue**. Seedha step-by-step samajhte hain:

### 1. Pehli Mistake: Business / Risk-Sizing Issue (11:08 AM wala SL hit)

Program ne `11:00 AM` ke aas-paas `25450PE` mein **`64.80` par entry le li thi** (rally ke bilkul starting breakout par). Lekin **11:08:53 par SL hit ho gaya (`56.60`)** aur `-₹588` ka loss hua.

* **Kyon hua?** Aapke chart par `11:06 AM` ke aas-paas ek normal red pullback candle aayi thi jo `66.85` se neeche `56.00` tak dip hui aur phir wahan se asli rally (`81.25` tak) shuru hui.


* **Asli Business Mistake:** Nifty ka 1 lot = `65` qty hai. Aapne `MAX_RISK_PER_TRADE = ₹500` rakha hai.

$$\frac{₹500}{65\text{ qty}} = \mathbf{₹7.69\text{ points}}$$



`₹65` ke option mein sirf `7.7` points ka SL (sirf ~11.8%) bahut tight hai — 3-minute candle ke ek normal wick mein ud jaata hai. Chart ka structural SL `52–53` (9/21 EMA / VWAP support) ke paas tha, jo ~12–13 points (`~₹800` risk) tha.

### 2. Doosri Mistake: Pure Code / Logical Issue (OTM Rotation ke baad Permanent Lockout)

Jab `11:08 AM` par `25450PE` ka SL hit hua, tab program ne kya kiya? Yeh sabse bada **Logical Bug** hai:

1. **Wrong Strike Jump (`25450PE` $\rightarrow$ `25500PE`):** SL hit hone par engine ne strike change karke **`25500PE`** (aur zyada ITM/expensive strike) ko active symbol bana liya.
2. **Phir `25500PE` bhaga (`₹84.90`):** Jab market aur gira aur `25500PE` `₹84.90` par pahuncha, tab aapke **OTM Rotation rule** ne kaha: *"Yeh strike mehnga ho gaya hai (`> ₹84.62`), wapas saste strike `25450PE` (`₹70.35`) par shift ho jao."*
3. **Yahan Logical Bug fasa:**
* Jab engine wapas `25450PE` par aaya, tab `25450PE` already **`₹70.35`** par chal raha tha (rally chal rahi thi).
* Lekin code ne `fetch_freeze_hour_base()` chalaya aur `10:15–11:00 AM` ka purana **Base High = `62.00**` load kar liya.
* Code ka **5% Chase Guard** kehta hai:

$$\text{Max Allowed Entry} = 62.00 \times 1.05 = \mathbf{65.10}$$


* Ab `25450PE` ki price `70.35` se upar (`72.85`, `78.15`, `72.55`, `81.25`) chal rahi thi. Har 3-minute candle par code bolta raha:
`[SKIP BREAKOUT] Close 72.85 > 5% Chase Guard (65.10)`
* **Matlab:** OTM rotation ne jis strike ko select kiya, uska current price (`70.35`) pehle se hi uske `Chase Guard (65.10)` se upar tha! Yani rotate hote hi wo strike **permanently block** ho gaya aur poori rally (`70` $\rightarrow$ `81.25`) sirf dekhta raha.
