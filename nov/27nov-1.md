### 1. Why the Opening Range Box Existed & Why We Can Shrink It

* **What it was doing:** From `9:15–9:30 AM`, NSE/BSE option bid-ask spreads and IV fluctuate wildly, and 9/21 EMAs need at least 15–20 candles (or prior-day history) to stabilize.
* **The Flaw:** Making `Close > Box_High` a **mandatory gate** meant that whenever a trend started early (`10:33 AM` at `47.95`), a late box (`10:15–11:00 AM`) forced the bot to sit on its hands and buy `35%` higher (`64.80`) after `11:00 AM`.


* **Minimum Viable Window (`15 Minutes`):** Let `9:15–9:30 AM` (just **five** 3-minute candles) serve as a lightweight **Initial Volatility Filter** (optional soft reference), and unlock active trading from **`9:30 AM` (or `10:00 AM` when your consulting setup finishes) to `1:30 PM**`.
